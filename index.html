PHAM NGUYEN STUDIO ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how STARBOUND ODYSSEY handles information when you use the game.

1. Information stored by the game

STARBOUND ODYSSEY does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, or other direct contact details to play.

The game stores gameplay information and preferences locally on your device. This may include game progress, scores, collected items, in-game currency, unlocked content, audio settings, and other gameplay settings. This information is stored using Android app storage and is used to operate the game and preserve your progress on that device.

We do not operate our own game account server or use the game code to send this locally stored gameplay information to our own server. If you uninstall the app or clear its storage, locally stored game data may be deleted.

2. Advertising and third-party services

The app integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to provide rewarded advertising. The app may also use Google Play's in-app review service.

When an ad is requested or displayed, Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect or receive information such as:

  • IP address and approximate location inferred from it;
  • device identifiers, including advertising identifiers where available;
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system, app version, and language;
  • ad interaction and diagnostic information, such as ad views, clicks, and performance events.

Google processes this information under its own privacy policies and may use it to provide, measure, prevent fraud in, and personalize advertising, subject to applicable settings, consent requirements, and law. We do not control Google's independent data processing.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Google Ads Settings

The app's advertising configuration may change. This policy will be updated if the services or data practices change.

3. Permissions

The app requests network access to load rewarded ads and check network connectivity. The game does not request access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or precise device location.

Third-party SDKs may process information as described in Section 2.

4. How information is used

  • save and restore gameplay progress and preferences on your device;
  • provide rewarded ads and related advertising functions;
  • maintain, protect, and improve the app;
  • diagnose advertising or technical issues and prevent fraud.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not operate our own service that shares your locally stored gameplay progress with third parties.

Information may be processed by Google and its service providers through the advertising and Google Play services described above. Their handling of information is governed by their own policies.

6. Data retention and deletion

Gameplay data stored locally remains on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. We do not receive a copy of this locally stored progress through our own game server.

Advertising-related information may be retained by Google according to Google's policies and retention practices. Please use the Google privacy and ad settings links above to review or manage available controls.

7. Children's privacy

STARBOUND ODYSSEY is not designed specifically to collect personal information from children. The game does not ask players to submit personal contact information. Advertising services may apply their own age-related and child-directed treatment where required by law and platform settings.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that personal information has been provided to us directly, please contact us using the details below so we can review the request. Do not send sensitive information in your message.

8. Security

We use Android's app storage for local game data and rely on Google to protect information processed through Google's services. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, its services, or applicable requirements change. The updated policy will be published at the same public URL, with a revised effective date.
